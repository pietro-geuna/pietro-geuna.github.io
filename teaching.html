Resources

Some resources that I found useful while preparing for the course:

  • A crash course in Good and Bad Controls.
  • Ben Lambert's graduate econometrics course.
  • 3Blue1Brown's Essence of Linear Algebra – useful if you're rusty with matrices (and, frankly, beautiful on its own).
